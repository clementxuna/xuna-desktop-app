XUNA AI for Windows

Your XUNA workspace in its own window, without a browser tab to lose. It always runs the newest XUNA, so there is nothing to update by hand.

Download for Windows

For Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. About 115 MB.

This installer is for Windows PCs. Open this page on your PC to install it, or use XUNA AI in your browser on this device.

Installing

  1. Open XUNA-AI-Setup.exe from your downloads. It installs in a few seconds, adds XUNA AI to your Start menu and desktop, and opens the app.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for apps that aren't code-signed yet.
  3. Sign in the same way you do on the web.

No Run anyway button? Smart App Control on that PC blocks apps that aren't code-signed. Use XUNA AI in your browser there for now.

Already installed? XUNA AI updates itself in the background.
